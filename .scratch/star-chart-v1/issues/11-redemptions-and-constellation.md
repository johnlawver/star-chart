# 11: Redemptions and Constellation

**What to build:** A grown-up records a **Redemption**, an amount and a note such as "ice cream", for a child. A Redemption larger than the Balance is refused. Each child's **Constellation**, every star earned and spent over time, appears on their Observatory view and in the Control Center.

**Blocked by:** 05 (Hand in and approve)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A grown-up can record a Redemption with an amount and a note. It reduces the Balance.
- [ ] A Redemption that would take the Balance below zero is refused.
- [ ] The Constellation lists earnings (from approvals) and Redemptions over time, in both the Control Center and on the child's Observatory view (kept plain).
- [ ] The Balance always equals earned minus spent (API test across several approvals and redemptions).
