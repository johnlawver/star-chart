# Units are set up through printed and scanned QR codes

The printer-scanner unit (an ESP32-S3 with a camera and a thermal printer) has no screen or keyboard, so it gets onto Wi-Fi and pairs using QR codes in both directions. It prints a Setup Guide QR that opens a setup page on the phone, and its camera reads a Handshake QR the phone shows. The Handshake QR carries the Wi-Fi network and password, the server's address and a one-time pairing code. We chose this over the usual ways of setting up such devices. A hotspot on the unit means switching the phone's Wi-Fi and serving a page without trusted HTTPS. Bluetooth setup needs Web Bluetooth, which Safari doesn't support, or a native app. The camera and printer are already there for tickets, and every phone can show and scan a QR code.

Phone browsers can't list Wi-Fi networks, so the unit scans for them itself and puts the names after the `#` in the Setup Guide's link, where they stay on the phone. The Wi-Fi password goes from the phone straight into the unit and never reaches the server. Because the handshake names the server, the same firmware works with any Star Chart server. Formats are in `.scratch/printer-unit/spec.md`.
